# Story research flow

1. `research/hopper.yaml` supplies broad candidate hypotheses.
2. Hopper Analysis ranks SSA curve anomalies from D1.
3. `research/next-batch-2025.md` records external evidence, precise theses, and advance/hold decisions.
4. `research/promoted-six-2025.yaml` is the handoff from discovery to story drafting.
5. Candidate SSA Snapshots downloads the official SSA archive, verifies the pinned archive SHA256, and emits reproducible snapshots for the promoted six.
6. Draft StorySpecs bind claims to those snapshots, pass `nbn story score`, and remain `draft` until explicit human approval.

Discovery score is not editorial approval. Temporal alignment with a cultural event is not treated as proof of causation.
