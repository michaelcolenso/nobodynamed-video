# Promoted six rationale

The next six were chosen to maximize story distinctiveness and defensibility, not raw hopper rank.

- **Rihanna:** clean culture-linked break; event timing and name shock align in 2005.
- **Miley:** unusual two-act curve; 2006 cultural launch plus a measurable recent comeback.
- **Aaliyah:** enormous 1994 break, followed by continued growth to a much later peak.
- **Elsa:** one of the tightest event/peak alignments in the hopper.
- **Leia:** event pulse in 1977 but peak in 2023; a built-in corrective to simplistic causation.
- **Atlas:** pure data counterweight; nineteenth-century history with an all-time peak in 2025.

Reserves are intentionally strong. Debra and Gary overlap existing generational-collapse stories, Ashley deserves a paired-sex StorySpec primitive, and Khaleesi is better held for a later culture-heavy batch.
