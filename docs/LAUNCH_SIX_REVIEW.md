Prepared 2026-09-08. **All six scripts are drafts awaiting human approval. No launch MP4s have been rendered or published.**

These packages replace the incorrect fixture-based pilot claims. The first four form the initial week; Jennifer and Eleanor are reserves. Twelve-and-a-half seconds is a target, not a measured narration duration. Final delivery must fit the 9–14 second envelope after natural speech, alignment, composition and audiovisual inspection.

## Release decisions

- Human approval must be recorded using `nbn story approve` only after the exact scripts and claims above are accepted. Approval binds the full story content; later edits invalidate it.
- Configure Workers AI credentials securely in the render environment. None were available in this workspace. Do not paste tokens into chat or commit them.
- Render `batches/launch-six.yaml`, inspect each narrated MP4 at phone size, check pronunciation and word alignment, then stage only the successful batch. Automated QC cannot judge the quality of a voice or historical interpretation.
- All legacy story files were returned to draft. The four old pilot paths carry corrected content as deprecated aliases; the other legacy scripts need independent verification before reuse.
- The draft PR must remain unmerged until release prerequisites are resolved: the main-branch workflow deliberately rejects unapproved launch scripts.

## Validation completed

Python regression tests, Ruff, strict mypy, Node tests/build and an actual synthetic Satori component smoke render were run. The component smoke uses a test fixture, not an approved launch story; it does not establish narration, complete MP4 quality or performance on TikTok.

## First-month growth experiment

Publish four verified videos per week; keep two ready as reserves. Treat this as a test cadence, not an algorithm requirement. Track two-second retention, average watch time relative to duration, completion, shares per 1,000 views, profile visits and follows per 1,000 views at consistent 24-hour and seven-day windows. Small samples are directional. After eight posts, repeat promising premises with fresh names; assess again after sixteen. Do not change the brand based on a single low-view post.

Use a searchable name/popularity phrase in the caption, a legible opening premise immediately, and one real audience question. Test hooks and story subjects before increasing visual complexity. No forecast of virality is implied. Published-video assessment remains open until individual post links or analytics are available.
